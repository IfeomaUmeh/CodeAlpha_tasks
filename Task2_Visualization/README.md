# Task 2: Data Visualization

Six charts built from the Superstore sales dataset (9,994 orders, 2014 to 2017) for the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

## Files
- `CodeAlpha_SuperstoreVisualization.ipynb`: the notebook with all charts and written insights
- `Sample - Superstore.csv`: the dataset, in the main folder of this repo

## Charts
1. Monthly sales trend, 2014 to 2017
2. Total profit by category
3. Sales and profit by region
4. Average profit by discount level
5. Profit heatmap by region and category
6. Top 5 and bottom 5 sub-categories by profit

## Key insights
- Sales peak between September and December every year
- Technology earns the most profit, while Furniture earns little despite strong sales
- West leads in both sales and profit
- Discounts of 30% and above turn the average order into a loss
- Central Furniture is the only loss-making region and category combination
- Tables and Bookcases are the sub-categories that lose the most money

## Tools
Python, pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab
